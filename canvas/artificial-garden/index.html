artificial garden

the garden grows from one root at the center. every piece of food in the soil pulls on the nearest part of it within reach, and each part that is pulled grows a short step toward its food. where food pulls one part two ways at once, it may split. nothing in the rules says where a branch goes, only what it grows toward.

a faint thread joins each piece of food to the part it pulls. food with no thread is out of reach, and waits. when nothing it can reach is left, the garden rests.

  • with food in hand, tap or click to drop some. the garden grows toward it if any part is close enough to feel it, so food out of reach waits for the garden to come nearer, and a trail of it leads the garden on.
  • with stones in hand, tap to place a stone, and branches grow around it. tap a stone to lift it.
  • without a pointer, the arrow keys move a ring over the plot, and enter drops food or places a stone there.
  • the presets show other soils and habits: islands of food out of reach, fine lace, a few long runners, and a six-fold mandala. the mandala keeps its own seed, because on about half of all seeds two parts are so nearly the same distance from a piece of food that rounding settles it differently in different arms, and they grow apart.
  • in controls, reach, growth, branching and wander change the rules while it grows. the food in the soil, its symmetry and the seed plant a new garden.
  • the same seed and settings always grow the same garden, until you feed it or place a stone. copy link, in controls, keeps them, so the link grows this garden again from the beginning, anywhere. it can't keep what you did while it grew: the food and stones you placed, or a rule you changed on the way. save image keeps the picture as it is now.

keys: space pause · r reset · n next seed · t food or stones · arrows move the ring, with shift faster · enter places at the ring · 1–5 presets · c copy link · s save image · esc closes a panel or hides the ring
