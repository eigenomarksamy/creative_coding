artificial garden

the garden grows from one root at the center. every piece of food in the soil pulls on the nearest part of it within reach, and each part that is pulled grows a short step toward its food. where food pulls one part two ways at once, it may split. nothing in the rules says where a branch goes, only what it grows toward.

when nothing it can reach is left, the garden rests.

  • with food in hand, tap or click to drop some. the garden grows toward it if any part is close enough to feel it, so food out of reach waits for the garden to come nearer.
  • with stones in hand, tap to place a stone, and branches grow around it. tap a stone to lift it.
  • the presets show other soils and habits, from fine lace to a few long runners.
  • in controls, reach, growth, branching and wander change the rules while it grows. the food in the soil, its symmetry and the seed plant a new garden.
  • the same seed and settings always grow the same garden, until you feed it or place a stone.

keys: space pause · r reset · n next seed · t food or stones · 1–4 presets · esc closes a panel
